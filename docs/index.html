ICT

FPL's own Influence/Creativity/Threat index, their official blend of how much a player affects match outcomes, creates chances, and threatens goal.

xGI

Expected Goal Involvements (expected goals + expected assists). Measures chance quality, not just results, the more predictive of the two stats.

Ownership bar

Scaled 0-35% (not 0-100%) so low-owned players are easy to compare. The red tick marks 10%, which is our cutoff for "still a real differential."

Score

Our own ranking: ICT + xGI, discounted the more owned a player gets, then adjusted up or down by their team's next 5 fixtures.
